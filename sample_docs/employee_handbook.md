# Brightwave Labs Employee Handbook

Brightwave Labs is a fictional company used for this demo. Welcome to the team.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may schedule your day freely. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your department head. Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 euros for a desk, chair or monitor.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year; the rest expire on March 31. Vacation requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be submitted at least 30 days in advance through the HR portal. Public holidays are additional and do not count against vacation days.

## Sick leave

Employees can take up to 10 paid sick days per year without a doctor's note. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days a medical certificate is required. Please notify your manager before 9:30 on the first day of sick leave.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 18 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birth parents and adoptive parents receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months.

## Expenses and travel

Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 euros per day. Submit expense reports within 30 days with receipts attached. Expenses above 1,000 euros need pre-approval from your manager.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 euros for courses, books and conferences. Conference attendance also includes up to two paid days of travel time.

## Security basics

Use the company password manager and enable two-factor authentication on all work accounts. Lost laptops or phones must be reported to the security team within 2 hours at security@brightwave.example. Never share customer data over personal messaging apps.
