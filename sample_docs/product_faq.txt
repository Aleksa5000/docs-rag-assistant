Brightwave Beacon - Product FAQ (fictional product, demo data)

Q: What is the Beacon?
The Beacon is a smart air-quality sensor that measures CO2, particulate matter (PM2.5), temperature and humidity. It sends readings to the Brightwave app every 60 seconds.

Q: How long does the battery last?
The Beacon has a rechargeable 4,000 mAh battery that lasts about 14 days on a single charge with default settings. Charging from empty to full takes 3 hours over USB-C. In power-saving mode, readings are sent every 5 minutes and the battery lasts up to 40 days.

Q: Is the Beacon waterproof?
The Beacon is splash resistant (IP54) but is not designed to be submerged or used outdoors in heavy rain. Do not place it in bathrooms with direct shower spray.

Q: Which Wi-Fi networks are supported?
The Beacon connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only. 5 GHz networks are not supported. Enterprise networks that require certificate login are not supported either.

Q: How do I reset the device?
Hold the round button on the back for 10 seconds until the LED blinks blue. This erases Wi-Fi settings but keeps your historical data, which is stored in your Brightwave account.

Q: How long is my data stored?
Free accounts keep 30 days of history. Plus accounts keep 2 years of history and can export readings as CSV.

Q: What does the red LED mean?
A red LED means CO2 is above 1,500 ppm and the room should be ventilated. A blinking red LED means the battery is below 10 percent.

Q: What is the warranty?
Every Beacon comes with a 24-month limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Damage from liquids or drops is not covered.

Q: Can I use the Beacon with Home Assistant?
Yes. The Beacon supports a local MQTT feed. Enable it under Settings, Integrations in the app. The Studio edition is not required for MQTT.
