# Brightwave Refund and Returns Policy

This policy applies to all purchases of Brightwave products, a fictional company used for this demo.

## Hardware returns

Brightwave hardware can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the device is in original condition with all accessories and packaging. Returns after 30 days but within 12 months are covered by the warranty and are repaired or replaced, not refunded. Return shipping is free for defective devices; for change-of-mind returns the customer pays the return shipping fee of 9.90 euros.

## Software subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the paid period. Annual subscriptions are refundable in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, annual subscriptions are refunded pro rata only if the cancellation is due to a service outage of more than 48 hours. Refunds are not available for usage-based credits that have already been consumed.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds for purchases made with gift cards are issued as store credit. We cannot refund to a different card or account than the one used for the purchase.

## Non-refundable items

Custom-engraved devices, opened software licence keys for the Studio edition and gift cards cannot be refunded unless required by law.

## How to request a refund

Contact support at refunds@brightwave.example with your order number, or use the Returns page in your account. Support responds within 1 business day. Large orders above 5,000 euros are reviewed by the finance team and can take up to 10 business days.
